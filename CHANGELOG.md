# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Open the search workspace from the toolbar. Supported desktop browsers show
  it in their native side panels.
- Choose, paste, or drop an image, enter a direct image link, or capture part of
  the active page in a supported side panel.
- Fresh installations select Google Lens, Bing Visual Search, Yandex Images, and
  TinEye. Six more providers are available under **Add engines** in Settings.
- The Chrome right-click menu groups **Open image**, **All search engines**, and
  individual engines. Fresh installations look for nearby images and alternate
  sizes of the image you clicked. Updates keep your saved preferences.
- Updated Sogou to use its current uploader for files and image links.

## 1.2.0

- Added a shared search workspace for desktop browser sidebars and a tab fallback.
- Simplified image upload, URL preview, area capture, and the new-search flow.
- Standardized the ten-engine catalog and four fresh-install defaults.
- Improved keyboard navigation, settings, and the right-click menu.
- Removed external support and donation features and assigned a separate Firefox add-on ID.
